LLM Setup

Configure the local Ollama model QueryBridge uses for schema chat and AI-assisted relationship discovery.

What the LLM receives

QueryBridge sends the current working schema, known/inferred relationships, and the knowledge entries you add. It does not require a live Databricks connection.

Schema ChatQuestions about tables, fields, joins and Databricks SQL.
AI Guess LinksSuggests equality joins and validates every proposed table/field against the captured schema.
KnowledgePackage notes, business rules and naming conventions are included as context.
